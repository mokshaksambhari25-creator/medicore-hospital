Clinical teams

Eight departments, one campus.

Walk in to emergency any hour. For clinics, ask reception or sign in as a patient to see the doctor already assigned to your file.

Emergency & Trauma

Triage, resuscitation and overnight observation. The desk never closes. Call 022 2416 2400 or 108.

Cardiology

Heart clinics, ECG and inpatient care for chest pain, follow-up and planned procedures. Dr. Meera Nair leads the floor.

Maternity

Antenatal visits, delivery suites and a quiet recovery floor. Family may wait in the corridor lounge.

Paediatrics

Doctors who treat children every day — from fever to planned surgery. Toys in the waiting bay, not in the ward.

Orthopaedics

Fractures, joints and physiotherapy after you leave the ward. X-ray is on the same floor as plaster.

Diagnostics

X-ray, CT, MRI and lab collection. Reports go to your doctor and into your patient login the same day when possible.

Neurology

Headache clinics, stroke watch and MRI planning. Ask reception for the next OPD slot.

Oncology

Day-care infusion, inpatient rooms and a quiet consult bay. Insurance desks sit next to billing.
