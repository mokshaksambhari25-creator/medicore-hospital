Visiting & emergency

A hospital that still feels human.

Emergency and ambulance run through the night. Daytime clinics are open for reviews, scans and new registrations. Ask at reception — they will walk you to the right ward.

Patients sign in to see their own reports and pay a bill. Hospital staff and the administrator use a separate desk.
