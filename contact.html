How to get a patient login

When you are registered, reception prints a card: your ID looks like P-4821. The password is your first name plus the last two digits of that ID. Staff use a doctor ID. The administrator uses ADMIN-1000.

If it is an emergency

Do not wait for an online booking. Come to the night porch or call 108. Triage will take you in; paperwork can wait.
