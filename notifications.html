Gmail — “setting not available”

Google hides App Passwords until 2-Step Verification is on. School/work accounts often block them. Use a personal Gmail.

  1. Open 2-Step Verification and turn it ON (phone SMS or Authenticator). Do this first — not App Passwords.
  2. Wait one minute. In the Google Account search box type App passwords.
  3. Create one named MediCore. Google shows 16 letters. Spaces are fine.
  4. Paste Gmail + that password below and Save. Then send a test to yourself.

Still “not available”? The Gmail is likely college/Workspace. Make a free personal @gmail.com and repeat. Outlook also works (smtp-mail.outlook.com) in smtp.env.
