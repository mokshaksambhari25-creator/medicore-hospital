MediCore — Gmail "setting not available"

Google message:
  "The setting that you are looking for is not available for your account."

Cause: App Passwords stay hidden until 2-Step Verification is ON,
or the account is school/work (Workspace) and the admin blocked them.

Fix (personal Gmail):
  1. https://myaccount.google.com/signinoptions/twosv
     Turn 2-Step Verification ON (phone number).
  2. Wait 1 minute.
  3. Google Account search box → type: App passwords
  4. Create "MediCore" → 16 letters.
  5. On the website: Email & SMS → paste Gmail + App Password → Save.

If it is still missing:
  - Use a personal @gmail.com, not college email.
  - Advanced Protection / passkey-only accounts cannot use App Passwords.
  - Alternative: Outlook SMTP in SERVER/smtp.env
      SMTP_HOST=smtp-mail.outlook.com
      SMTP_PORT=587
      SMTP_USER=you@outlook.com
      SMTP_PASSWORD=your-outlook-password
      SMTP_FROM=you@outlook.com

Never put smtp.env on GitHub.
